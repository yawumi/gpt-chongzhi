# ChatGPT Plus Top-Up Guide (2026): USDT, Apple Pay & Google Pay

[简体中文](README.md) | **English**

You can purchase a ChatGPT Plus activation code with **USDT**, or pay with **Apple Pay or Google Pay through the USD payment option**. This guide explains how to place an order, retrieve your code, and redeem it on your own ChatGPT account.

The walkthrough uses V3 Shop and follows the process from checkout to activation. It also covers ChatGPT Pro and Claude Pro renewals later in the guide.

**Choose a Plus product → Pay with USDT or select USD for Apple Pay / Google Pay → Retrieve your code → Verify the code → Confirm your account → Submit → Check your subscription.**

Updated October 25, 2026. Check the product page when ordering for current prices, stock, and renewal conditions.

## 1. What you need before topping up ChatGPT Plus

Have these ready:

1. **Your own ChatGPT account:** sign in through your browser and check the email address and current plan.
2. **An email address you can access:** use it for order contact details and lookup.
3. **Your preferred payment method:** a USDT wallet, or a device and browser with Apple Pay or Google Pay set up.

A desktop browser makes it easier to keep the store, ChatGPT, and the recharge page open in separate tabs. You can also follow the steps in a mobile browser.

If you already have Plus, check when it expires. You will need that information when choosing a renewal product.

## 2. How to buy a Plus activation code with USDT, Apple Pay or Google Pay

### Step 1: Open the store and choose a ChatGPT Plus product

Open [V3 Shop in English](https://shop.gpt.ge/en) and find the products labeled **Plus top-up**. Open the product details and confirm that you are buying a ChatGPT Plus membership top-up.

The store currently lists two channels with different renewal conditions:

| Product channel | What to check before ordering |
| --- | --- |
| Philippine card / Fica | The current product requires an existing Plus subscription to expire before renewal. |
| iOS | The current product allows a replacement top-up, but remaining subscription time does not stack. If you previously used this channel, review its renewal instructions first. |

For one account and a one-month product, the quantity is normally **1**. Check the duration and stock before proceeding.

### Step 2: Enter your contact email and set a lookup password

Complete the fields in the purchase form:

- **Contact Email:** enter an address you can access. You can use it to find the order later.
- **Lookup Password:** create a separate pickup code for this order and save it. You will need it to retrieve the activation code.
- **CAPTCHA:** enter the digits shown on the page.

The lookup password protects your store order. **You will confirm the ChatGPT account to receive the membership later, in the recharge center.**

### Step 3: Choose USDT, or select USD for Apple Pay / Google Pay

Under **Payment Method**, choose the button for the payment route you want:

| How you want to pay | Button to select on the product page | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USDT (Tether) | **USDT** | Follow the crypto checkout instructions. |
| Apple Pay | **USD** — the **USD支付** payment entry | Choose Apple Pay at the payment provider's checkout. |
| Google Pay | **USD** — the **USD支付** payment entry | Choose Google Pay at the payment provider's checkout. |

**For Apple Pay or Google Pay, start with the USD button.** The wallet options are available through that checkout route.

After selecting the payment method:

1. Click **Buy Now**.
2. Review the product, quantity, amount, payment method, and contact email in the order confirmation window.
3. Confirm payment to open the checkout page.
4. Complete payment using the instructions below.

#### Paying with USDT

At the crypto checkout, check the **required network, receiving address, and exact USDT amount**. In your wallet, use the same network and enter the payment details shown for this order. Follow any payment time limit displayed by the checkout.

Keep the transaction hash until the order is confirmed as paid. If your wallet shows a completed transfer while the store is still waiting, check the existing payment status before making another transfer.

#### Paying with Apple Pay or Google Pay

After choosing **USD**, open the payment provider's checkout and select **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay**. Review the amount and currency, then authorize the payment using your wallet.

Wallet buttons depend on the device, browser, and wallet setup. If the button you need is missing, first check that you selected **USD** and opened the checkout on a device and browser where that wallet is available.

When payment finishes, wait for the order page to open and save your order number. If the automatic return does not happen, use Order Lookup as described below.

## 3. How to retrieve your activation code after payment

### Step 4: Use your lookup password to unlock the code

On the order page, check that the payment status is **Paid**, then find the section containing the delivered code.

If the page asks for a password:

1. Enter the **Lookup Password** you created when ordering.
2. Use the code lookup button to retrieve the delivery details.
3. Read the activation code and the accompanying usage instructions.
4. Copy and save the code, along with the recharge address provided in the order.

Keep both your order number and activation code: the order number is used for order lookup and support, while the activation code is used to redeem the membership.

If you closed the payment page, open [Order Lookup](https://shop.gpt.ge/en/order), search by **order number or checkout email**, and retrieve the code from the matching order.

**Payment completes the purchase of the code. Continue below to activate the membership on your ChatGPT account.**

## 4. How to redeem the code on your own ChatGPT account

### Step 5: Open the recharge center and verify your Plus code

Follow your order instructions to the [AI Recharge Center](https://ai.gpt.ge/). Use the language switch at the top to select **English** if needed.

The ChatGPT page has three stages: **Verify Card → Get Token → Submit**.

Paste your activation code into the **Activation code** field and click **Verify**. Once verified, check that the detected product matches the Plus membership you purchased.

Copy only the code itself, without the order number, surrounding instructions, or extra spaces.

### Step 6: Get your account information and enter the JSON Token

At the **Get Token** stage:

1. In the **same browser**, sign in to the ChatGPT account you want to top up. The recharge page provides an **Open ChatGPT login** button.
2. Check that the signed-in email belongs to the intended account.
3. Return to the recharge page and click **Fetch and copy your token**.
4. The new page displays your account session information as JSON. Copy the **complete JSON content**.
5. Return to the recharge page, paste it into **JSON Token**, and continue.

Keep the opening and closing braces and all fields. An email address, screenshot, or partial extract will not satisfy the JSON input requirement. If your particular code presents different account fields, follow the instructions shown for those fields.

Token / Session JSON contains account access credentials. Submit it only if you trust the recharge service, and keep it out of messages, screenshots, and public repositories.

### Step 7: Confirm the account email and submit the top-up

On the confirmation screen, check the **Recharge email** and product type. This is especially useful if you have signed in to several ChatGPT accounts in the same browser.

If the page detects an existing membership and asks about replacing it, review what will happen to the remaining time before continuing.

Click **Confirm** to submit. If the page requests a security verification, complete it in the window provided and return to the original page to check the result.

While the top-up is processing, keep your original code and order details. Check that existing request's status if the result is delayed.

### Step 8: Check that Plus is active in ChatGPT

After the recharge center reports success, return to ChatGPT, refresh the page, and check the current plan in your account settings. Confirm that the intended account shows Plus and review its subscription status.

If the recharge page reports success but your account has not updated, sign in again and recheck. If the subscription still has not appeared after 30 minutes, keep the order number, payment proof, and recharge result for support.

## 5. How to top up ChatGPT Pro and Claude Pro

### ChatGPT Pro activation and renewal

Choose the ChatGPT Pro product in the store and check its current plan, availability, and ordering instructions. Some products may require a different ordering process described on their page.

For a self-service activation code, payment and code retrieval follow the same steps above. In the recharge center, verify that the code is recognized as **Pro**, provide the requested account information, check the email, and submit.

If you are upgrading from Plus, first read the product's instructions about upgrades and remaining subscription time. Confirm the resulting plan and available usage in your own account.

### Claude Pro activation and renewal

Purchase the corresponding Claude Pro product and retrieve its code. The account step then uses your Claude Organization ID:

1. Open the recharge center and switch to **Claude Pro** at the top.
2. Enter the Claude code and click **Verify**.
3. At the account binding stage, click **Open Claude settings to get Organization ID**.
4. Sign in to the intended Claude account, copy its Organization ID from account settings, and paste it into the recharge form.
5. Click **Bind account**, then **Submit recharge**.
6. Wait for the result, then check the Pro subscription in your Claude account.

The current Claude product describes replacement renewals. Check how remaining time is handled if you already have a subscription, and confirm stock before purchasing.

## 6. Frequently asked questions

### Can I pay for ChatGPT Plus with USDT?

Yes. For the flow in this guide, choose **USDT** on the V3 Shop product page, complete the crypto checkout, and retrieve your activation code. Then redeem it through the recharge center. Use the network and payment details shown for your order.

### Where are the Apple Pay and Google Pay buttons?

Choose **USD** on the product page first. This is the **USD支付** entry for Apple Pay and Google Pay checkout. The wallet buttons appear at the payment provider's checkout when supported by your device, browser, and wallet setup.

### Does this payment happen on the ChatGPT website?

You pay V3 Shop for an activation code, then use the recharge center to apply it to your ChatGPT account. The USDT and USD payment choices described here belong to the store's checkout.

### I paid, but my ChatGPT account still does not show Plus. What next?

Check which stage you have completed. Paying for the order delivers a code; you still need to **retrieve it, verify it, confirm your account, and submit the top-up**. Follow Sections 3 and 4 to finish.

If you already submitted it, check the recharge result and the subscription status of the intended account.

### My USDT transfer is complete, but the order is still unpaid. What should I check?

Compare the network, recipient address, and amount with the checkout instructions, then check the payment status. Keep your transaction hash and order number. If the order remains unpaid, provide those details to store support so they can investigate the existing payment.

### How much does a ChatGPT Plus top-up cost?

Check the current product price, duration, and quantity before ordering. Philippine card and iOS products can have different prices. Review the final amount and currency at checkout; the store notes that currency conversion can cause a small difference from the displayed product price.

### Can I renew Plus before my current subscription expires?

It depends on the product. The current Philippine card option requires the existing subscription to expire first. The iOS option allows a replacement top-up, but remaining time does not stack. If you want to use all your current subscription time, wait until it expires before following the renewal instructions.

### Where do I enter the activation code?

Enter it in the **recharge center provided with your order** and click **Verify**. Continue with the account information requested there. Your order number and lookup password serve different purposes and cannot replace the activation code.

### What if the page says my JSON Token is invalid?

Confirm that you are signed in to the intended ChatGPT account in the same browser. Click **Fetch and copy your token** again and copy the latest complete JSON, including its braces and all fields. Check for missing content or a single copied field where the full JSON is required.

### What if I forget my lookup password?

The lookup password is the pickup code you created for the store order. Check your saved order information. If you cannot find it, keep your order number and payment proof ready and ask store support to help verify the order.

### Do I need to send my ChatGPT password to support?

In this workflow, you sign in to ChatGPT yourself in your browser. The store uses a separate lookup password, while the recharge center requests account information for the selected product. Treat the Token used in the standard flow as sensitive account access information, as explained in Section 4.

### Is a Plus subscription the same as an API balance top-up?

Choose a Plus or Pro membership product to activate a subscription on your ChatGPT account. The store also lists a separate relay API balance product for applications and developers; its code is redeemed into the gpt.ge API platform wallet. Check which product matches your intended use before ordering.

### Can I buy a code now and redeem it much later?

Buy near the time you intend to use it, then redeem and check the result promptly. The store advises against keeping Plus and Pro codes unused for more than seven days, and individual products may have stricter requirements.

### Can I get an invoice for an Apple Pay or Google Pay purchase?

The store states that orders paid through the **USD payment route** automatically receive an overseas Invoice. Apple Pay and Google Pay use that route in this guide. Check the order's invoice details and your organization's reimbursement requirements; for other payment methods, review their invoicing terms separately.

### How do I get help with a failed top-up or a lost subscription?

Keep your order number, payment record, and error message. Contact [store support](https://shop.gpt.ge/en/contact) and explain whether the problem happened during payment, code retrieval, or recharge submission. Mask complete activation codes and Tokens in screenshots.

Support periods and remedies vary by product, so refer to the terms shown when you purchased. For order and delivery instructions, see the [Purchase Help & FAQ](https://shop.gpt.ge/en/help).
